🌴 Event Rules

  • 💶Pricing: Online tickets cost 5 € per person (group of 3: 12 € total, i.e. 4 € each). At the door (Abendkasse) the price is 7 € for everyone — no exceptions and no group deal at the door.
  • 🎓Students & residents' friends only. You must be an enrolled student at a Darmstadt university, or personally invited by a Ketteler resident, to attend.
  • 🤝Respect everyone. Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated. Treat all guests with respect.
  • 💳Refund policy. If you can't attend, let us know at least 24 hours before the event and we'll refund you in full — valid for all tickets except the group deal (3 × 4 €), which is non-refundable. No-shows are non-refundable.
