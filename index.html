🌴 Event Rules

  • 🎓Students only. You must be an enrolled student at a Darmstadt university to attend.
  • 🍺Alcohol responsibly. No excessive drinking. Anyone visibly intoxicated may be asked to leave.
  • 🤝Respect everyone. Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated. Treat all guests with respect.
  • 🔇Noise limits. We respect our neighbours. Music stops at the agreed time. No loud noise in corridors.
  • 🧹Keep it clean. Help keep the common areas tidy. Report any damage immediately.
  • 💳Refund policy. If you can't attend, let us know at least 24 hours before the event and we'll refund you in full. No-shows are non-refundable.
  • 📵Photos & privacy. Ask before taking photos of others. No posting without consent.
  • 🚪Organisers' decision is final. The organising team reserves the right to ask anyone to leave.
